The One-Paragraph Take

CMS is distributing $50B over five years to all 50 states for rural health transformation. State plans explicitly name consumer wearables, remote patient monitoring, and workforce wellness — three categories where WHOOP is a structural fit, not a stretch. Most state procurement windows are still open. The window to embed WHOOP as the standard for continuous physiologic monitoring in rural health closes inside 18 months. We have a state-by-state portfolio in motion to do exactly that.

The Opportunity

A federal program designed to fund exactly what WHOOP builds.

The Rural Health Transformation Program is a CMS cooperative agreement. Each state designed its own multi-initiative plan — workforce, chronic disease, technology, capital, behavioral health — and now controls how the money flows to rural hospitals, critical access hospitals, FQHCs, and the vendors that serve them.

The Funder

CMS Office of Rural Health Transformation

Federal cooperative agreements. $10B per year through FY30. Awards already issued; states are now operationalizing.

The Buyer

State Health Departments

Each state runs its own playbook through agencies like HHSC, HCAI, DOH, DHHS — and pushes funding to ~1,800 rural hospitals and clinics nationwide.

The Vendor Lane

WHOOP

State plans explicitly name consumer wearables, remote patient monitoring, and workforce wellness as fundable categories. That language maps directly to our platform.

The Problem CMS Is Trying to Solve

Rural America is in a healthcare crisis. This is the response.

More than 130 rural hospitals have closed in the past decade. Forty-six million Americans live in rural counties — disproportionately older, sicker, and underinsured. Diabetes, cardiovascular disease, behavioral health crises, and maternal mortality all run higher than the national average, while the workforce keeps shrinking.

The result is a system where the patients who need continuous care the most are the hardest to reach. CMS designed RHT to put $50B against that gap — and to do it through technology, workforce, and care-delivery models that scale beyond brick-and-mortar visits.

01

Chronic disease management at scale

Diabetes, hypertension, heart failure, CKM Syndrome — the conditions that drive most rural mortality and cost.

02

Remote patient monitoring

The patient is 60 miles from the clinic. Continuous data has to come to the provider, not the other way around.

03

Workforce wellness and retention

Rural hospitals can't keep clinicians. Workforce dollars now include wellness, recovery, and burnout interventions.

Why WHOOP

Continuous physiology is the missing data layer in rural care.

Rural patients don't show up to clinic weekly. EMRs see them in flashes. WHOOP delivers the longitudinal cardiovascular, sleep, recovery, and behavioral signal that an RPM program actually needs — in a form patients will actually wear.

Most RPM tools are single-purpose: a cuff, a glucose monitor, a pulse-ox. WHOOP is the only consumer-grade device that captures cardiovascular, sleep, recovery, and behavioral signal in one platform — built for enterprise deployment.
